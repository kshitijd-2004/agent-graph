# Q3 Risk Assessment

## Top Risks

1. **[HIGH] Customer concentration risk** — Top 10 customers = 42% of revenue. Loss of any single enterprise account would materially impact Q4.
2. **[MEDIUM] APAC regulatory changes** — New data localization laws in Japan and India may require infrastructure changes by Q1 next year.
3. **[MEDIUM] Key person dependency** — Lead architect holds 60% of platform domain knowledge. No documented succession plan.
4. **[LOW] Currency fluctuation** — EUR/USD and JPY/USD volatility affects reported revenue.
5. **[LOW] Competitor pricing pressure** — Two competitors launched discount programs in Q3.

## Mitigation Status
- Risk 1: Diversification program underway (target: 35% by Q4)
- Risk 2: Legal review in progress
- Risk 3: Documentation sprint planned for Q4
- Risk 4: Hedging policy under review
- Risk 5: Price matching capability being developed
