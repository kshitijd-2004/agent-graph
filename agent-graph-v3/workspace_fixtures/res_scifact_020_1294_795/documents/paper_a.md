# Paper A: Mitochondria: Dynamic Organelles in Disease, Aging, and Development

Source: peer-reviewed abstract (SciFact corpus, doc 8551160).

## Abstract

Mitochondria are the primary energy-generating system in most eukaryotic cells. Additionally, they participate in intermediary metabolism, calcium signaling, and apoptosis. Given these well-established functions, it might be expected that mitochondrial dysfunction would give rise to a simple and predictable set of defects in all tissues. However, mitochondrial dysfunction has pleiotropic effects in multicellular organisms. Clearly, much about the basic biology of mitochondria remains to be understood.

## Conclusion

Here we discuss recent work that suggests that the dynamics (fusion and fission) of these organelles is important in development and disease.
