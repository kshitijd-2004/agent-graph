Pension expense decreased from the prior year due to lower pension settlements, lower loss amortization, primarily from favorable asset experience and the impact of higher discount rates, partially offset by lower expected returns on assets. Special items (settlements, termination benefits, and curtailments) decreased from the prior year primarily due to lower pension settlement losses.
