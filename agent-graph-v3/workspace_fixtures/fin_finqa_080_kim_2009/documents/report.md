Latin american investments during 2009, the company acquired a land parcel located in rio clara, brazil through a newly formed consolidated joint venture in which the company has a 70% (70%) controlling ownership interest for a purchase price of 3.3 million brazilian reals (approximately usd $ 1.5 million). This parcel will be developed into a 48000 square foot retail shopping center. Additionally, during 2009, the company acquired a land parcel located in san luis potosi, mexico, through an unconsolidated joint venture in which the company has a noncontrolling interest, for an aggregate purchase price of approximately $ 0.8 million.
