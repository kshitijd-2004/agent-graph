Because of the interrelation of applied 's operations, properties within a country may be shared by the segments operating within that country. Products in the silicon systems group are manufactured in austin, texas; gloucester, massachusetts; rehovot, israel; and singapore. Remanufactured products in the applied global services segment are produced primarily in austin, texas. Products in the display segment are manufactured in santa clara, california; alzenau, germany; and tainan, taiwan. Products in the energy and environmental solutions segment are primarily manufactured in alzenau, germany; cheseaux, switzerland; and treviso, italy. In addition to the above properties, applied leases office space for marketing, sales, engineering and customer support offices in 79 locations throughout the world: 17 in europe, 23 in japan, 16 in north america (principally the united states), 7 in china, 7 in korea, 6 in southeast asia, and 3 in taiwan. Applied also owns 112 acres of buildable land in texas that could accommodate approximately 1708000 square feet of additional building space, 12.5 acres in california that could accommodate approximately 400000 square feet of additional building space, 10.8 acres in massachusetts that could accommodate approximately 65000 square feet of additional building space and 10 acres in israel that could accommodate approximately 111000 square feet of additional building space. Applied also leases 4 acres in italy that could accommodate approximately 180000 square feet of additional building space. Applied considers the properties that it owns or leases as adequate to meet its current and future requirements.
