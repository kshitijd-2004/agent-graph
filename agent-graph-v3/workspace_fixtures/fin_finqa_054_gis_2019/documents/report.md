Any amounts reflected on the consolidated balance sheets as accounts payable and accrued liabilities are excluded from the table above. Other long-term obligations mainly consist of liabilities for accrued compensation and benefits, including the underfunded status of certain of our defined benefit pension, other postretirement benefit, and postemployment benefit plans, and miscellaneous liabilities.
