Cash from operating activities remained strong throughout the 2011 to 2013 time period. Operating cash flow was favorably impacted in 2013, compared with 2012, by lower contributions into our defined benefit pension and postretirement benefit plans; however, this was partially offset by certain tnt express transaction-related charges, as well as changes in income tax receivables and payables. We paid a termination fee to tnt express of 20ac200 million ($ 268 million) under the agreement to terminate the merger protocol in the first quarter of 2013. Additionally, the cash payments for income taxes increased in 2013 compared with 2012, and were impacted by the timing of current tax deductions.
